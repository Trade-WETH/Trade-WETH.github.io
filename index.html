What is Trade WETH?

WETH is Wrapped Ether, the ERC-20 representation of ETH used across Ethereum DeFi; ethereum.org explains why wrapping makes ETH work with ERC-20 contracts.

Before you trade

Verified contract first

Use the Ethereum mainnet WETH contract shown on Etherscan, not a copied ticker from search or social posts.

App-first routing

Uniswap supports prefilled swap links, so the page can send users directly into the official web app with WETH selected.

ETH and WETH are different forms

ETH pays gas natively, while WETH is the ERC-20 wrapper used by many token contracts, DEX pools, and DeFi apps.

Trade WETH FAQ

Where can I trade weth?

You can use the official Uniswap web app route on app.uniswap.org and verify WETH against the Ethereum mainnet contract before connecting a wallet.

Do I need ETH to trade weth?

Yes. Ethereum transactions require ETH for gas, even when the asset you receive or approve is WETH.

Which contract should I verify before I trade weth?

On Ethereum mainnet, verify 0xC02aaA39b223FE8D0A0e5C4F27eAD9083C756Cc2 on Etherscan.

Is WETH the same as ETH when I trade weth?

WETH represents ETH in ERC-20 form, but it is still a token contract interaction; review approvals, network, and wallet prompts carefully.

Can I trade weth on other networks?

Yes, but contract addresses differ by network. Always verify the address on the relevant official explorer before sending funds or approving a swap.
